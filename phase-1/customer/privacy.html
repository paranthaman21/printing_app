Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 11, 2026

At Barath Store, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your data when you use our Online Print Pickup System.

In Simple Terms: We only collect the minimum information needed to process your print orders. We don't sell your data, we don't spam you, and we delete your files after printing.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

When you place an order, we collect:

  • Name: To identify your order at pickup
  • Phone Number (Optional): For order notifications (if you choose to provide it)
  • Uploaded Files: The documents you want to print (PDF, DOC, DOCX)
  • Print Preferences: Color/B&W, number of copies, page range
  • Payment Information: UPI transaction ID (if paying online)

1.2 Information Automatically Collected

  • Order ID: Unique identifier for your order
  • Timestamp: Date and time of order placement
  • IP Address: For security and fraud prevention
  • Browser Information: To ensure website compatibility
  • Device Type: Mobile or desktop (for analytics)

1.3 Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Email address (unless you voluntarily provide it)
  • Home address or detailed personal information
  • Credit/debit card numbers (we use UPI, not card payments)
  • Social media profiles or accounts
  • Browsing history outside our website

2. How We Use Your Information

Data Type Purpose Retention Period
Name Order identification at pickup 7 days after order completion
Phone Number Order notifications (optional) 7 days after order completion
Uploaded Files Printing your documents Deleted within 7 days or after pickup
Payment Details Payment verification & accounting 7 years (as per Indian tax laws)
Order History Business analytics & dispute resolution 1 year

2.1 Specific Use Cases

  • Order Processing: To print and prepare your documents
  • Payment Verification: To confirm UPI payments have been received
  • Customer Support: To resolve issues or answer questions
  • Legal Compliance: To maintain records as required by Indian tax laws
  • Service Improvement: To analyze usage patterns and improve our service
  • Fraud Prevention: To detect and prevent fraudulent orders

3. File Storage & Security

3.1 Where Your Files Are Stored

We use Supabase, a secure cloud storage service, to temporarily store your uploaded files:

  • Location: Supabase servers (may be located outside India)
  • Encryption: Files are transmitted using HTTPS (SSL/TLS encryption)
  • Access Control: Only authorized shop staff can access files
  • Storage Duration: Files are automatically deleted within 7 days or after order completion

3.2 Database Storage

Order information (name, phone, order details, payment status) is stored in a Supabase PostgreSQL database:

  • Protected by authentication and access controls
  • Regular backups for data integrity
  • Compliant with industry-standard security practices

3.3 Security Measures

  • HTTPS Encryption: All data transmission is encrypted
  • Secure Authentication: Shop admin access is password-protected
  • Session Management: Automatic logout after inactivity
  • Regular Updates: We keep our software up-to-date with security patches
  • Limited Access: Only essential personnel can access customer data
Important: While we implement strong security measures, no system is 100% secure. You upload files at your own risk. Do not upload highly sensitive or confidential documents.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

4.1 Automatic Deletion

  • Uploaded Files: Deleted within 7 days or immediately after order completion
  • Personal Information: Name and phone deleted after 7 days
  • Order Records: Kept for 1 year for business analytics
  • Payment Records: Kept for 7 years (Indian tax law requirement)

4.2 Manual Deletion Requests

You have the right to request deletion of your data:

  • Contact us at vbarath210706@gmail.com with your Order ID
  • We will delete your personal information within 7 working days
  • Payment records may be retained for legal compliance (tax laws)
  • Anonymized data may be retained for analytics (no personal identifiers)

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

5.1 Who We Share Data With

  • Supabase: Cloud storage and database provider (necessary for service operation)
  • UPI Payment Providers: Banks and payment gateways (for payment processing)
  • Legal Authorities: If required by law or court order

5.2 Who We DO NOT Share Data With

  • Marketing companies or advertisers
  • Data brokers or analytics firms
  • Social media platforms
  • Any third party for commercial purposes
We Never Sell Your Data: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to anyone. Period.

6. Your Rights Under Indian Law

Under the Information Technology Act, 2000 and related rules, you have the following rights:

6.1 Right to Access

  • You can request a copy of your personal data we hold
  • Contact us with your Order ID to request your data

6.2 Right to Correction

  • You can request correction of inaccurate information
  • Contact us to update your name or phone number

6.3 Right to Deletion

  • You can request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal requirements)
  • We will comply within 7 working days

6.4 Right to Withdraw Consent

  • You can withdraw consent for data processing at any time
  • Note: This may prevent us from completing your order

6.5 Right to Complain

  • If you believe your privacy rights have been violated, contact us first
  • You can also file a complaint with the relevant Indian authorities

7. Cookies & Tracking

7.1 Cookies We Use

7.2 Cookies We Do NOT Use

7.3 Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but this may affect website functionality (e.g., shopkeeper login won't work).
