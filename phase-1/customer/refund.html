Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last Updated: February 11, 2026

At Barath Store, we strive to provide excellent service. This policy explains when refunds and cancellations are available for our Online Print Pickup System.

Quick Summary: You can cancel before we start printing. Once printed, we cannot offer refunds unless there's a printing error on our part. Always verify your prints before leaving the shop.

1. Cancellation Policy

1.1 Before Printing Starts

You can cancel your order FREE OF CHARGE if:

  • The order has not yet been marked as "Processing" by shop staff
  • We have not started printing your documents
  • You contact us immediately after placing the order

How to Cancel:

  • Visit the shop in person and show your Order ID/QR code
  • Call us at [Shop Phone Number] with your Order ID
  • Email vbarath210706@gmail.com with your Order ID

1.2 After Printing Starts

No Cancellations: Once we have started printing your documents, the order cannot be cancelled. You will be responsible for payment.

1.3 Cancellation Timeline

Order Status Can Cancel? Refund Amount
Pending (Not Started) ✅ Yes 100% refund (if paid)
Processing (Printing) ❌ No No refund
Ready for Pickup ❌ No No refund
Completed ❌ No No refund

2. Refund Policy

2.1 Eligible Refund Scenarios

✅ Scenario 1: Order Cancelled Before Printing

Situation: You placed an order and paid via UPI, but immediately realized you uploaded the wrong file.

Solution: Contact us within 5 minutes. If we haven't started printing, we'll cancel and refund 100%.

Refund Time: 3-7 working days to your UPI account.

✅ Scenario 2: Printing Error by Shop

Situation: We printed the wrong pages, wrong number of copies, or wrong color mode.

Solution: We will reprint correctly at no additional charge OR provide a full refund.

Condition: You must report this before leaving the shop.

✅ Scenario 3: Duplicate Payment

Situation: You accidentally paid twice for the same order.

Solution: Provide both transaction IDs. We'll refund the duplicate payment.

Refund Time: 7 working days.

✅ Scenario 4: Payment Deducted but Order Failed

Situation: Your UPI payment was deducted but the order was not created in our system.

Solution: Provide transaction screenshot and UPI reference number. We'll verify and refund.

Refund Time: 7-10 working days after verification.

✅ Scenario 5: Shop Closure / Unforeseen Circumstances

Situation: Shop is closed due to emergency and we cannot fulfill your order.

Solution: Full refund if order cannot be completed within 24 hours.

Refund Time: 7 working days.

2.2 Non-Refundable Scenarios

❌ No Refunds in These Cases:
❌ Scenario 1: Change of Mind After Printing

Situation: We printed your documents correctly, but you no longer need them.

Policy: No refund. You must still pay and collect the prints.

Reason: We have already incurred printing costs (paper, ink, labor).

❌ Scenario 2: Poor Quality Source File

Situation: The print quality is poor because your uploaded file was low resolution.

Policy: No refund. We print exactly what you upload.

Prevention: Always check your file quality before uploading.

❌ Scenario 3: Unclaimed Orders

Situation: You paid for an order but never came to collect it.

Policy: No refund after 7 days. Unclaimed prints will be disposed of.

Reason: We held the prints for you and cannot resell them.

❌ Scenario 4: Complaints After Leaving Shop

Situation: You collected your prints, left the shop, and later complained about quality.

Policy: No refund. You must verify prints before leaving.

Reason: We cannot verify if the issue occurred after you left.

❌ Scenario 5: Wrong File Uploaded

Situation: You uploaded the wrong file and we printed it correctly.

Policy: No refund. You are responsible for uploading the correct file.

Solution: You can place a new order for the correct file.

4. Special Circumstances

4.1 Technical Issues

If our website or payment system malfunctions:

  • We will honor your order at the quoted price
  • If unable to fulfill, we will provide a full refund
  • We are not liable for losses beyond the order amount

4.2 Printer Malfunction

If our printer breaks down after you've paid:

  • Option 1: Wait for repair (usually within 24 hours) - No extra charge
  • Option 2: Full refund if you cannot wait

4.3 Power Outage / Natural Disasters

  • We will complete your order as soon as power is restored
  • If delay exceeds 24 hours, you can request a full refund
  • We are not liable for consequential damages due to delays

4.4 Incorrect Pricing Display

If there's an error in our pricing system:

  • We reserve the right to cancel the order and refund in full
  • We will contact you before cancelling
  • You can choose to pay the correct price or receive a refund

5. Partial Refunds

5.1 When Partial Refunds Apply

  • Partial Printing Error: If only some pages are misprinted, we refund only for those pages
  • Partial Order Cancellation: If you ordered 5 copies but only want 3, we refund for 2 copies (if we haven't printed yet)

5.2 Calculation

Partial refund = (Number of affected pages × Rate per page)

Example: You ordered 100 B&W pages. We misprinted 10 pages.

  • Refund = 10 pages × ₹2 = ₹20
  • OR we reprint the 10 pages for free
